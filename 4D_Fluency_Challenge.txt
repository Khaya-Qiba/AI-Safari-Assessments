Interaction A: Science Tutoring (Photosynthesis)
Delegation: Teacher introduces local plant examples such as maize or sukuma wiki. AI provides step-by-step explanation in simple language, reinforcing the teacher’s input.
Description: Product – clear explanation of photosynthesis aligned with Kenya CBC syllabus. Process – stepwise breakdown using maize leaves as the example. Performance – student explains the process back in their own words.
Discernment: AI checks against Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) resources before responding.
Temperature: Low, to ensure consistent and factual delivery.
RAG: Retrieval pulls verified KICD science notes to prevent hallucinations.
Negative Prompting: Do not use terms like “snow” or “winter.”
Interaction B: Math Homework Help (Algebra)
Agency Modality: Autonomous Ranger – AI acts as a guided problem-solver, not a full solution provider. Students in low-bandwidth areas receive step-by-step hints instead of full video explanations.
Knowledge Boundaries: AI limits scope to curriculum-aligned algebra for Uganda PLE and Kenya CBC. Avoids advanced or irrelevant topics.
Behavior Patterns: Provides one hint at a time, waits for student input, then continues. Encourages peer discussion after each step.
Temperature: Low, to ensure clarity and avoid unnecessary complexity.
RAG: Links to official exam past papers and curriculum guides to confirm accuracy.
Negative Prompting: Do not reference Western currency such as dollars or pounds in examples.
Interaction C: Parent Progress Report
Diligence Principles: Bias Awareness – AI avoids cultural bias and reports only on actual quiz scores. Transparency – AI states clearly that the summary is based on recorded quiz results, not predictions. Fact-Checking – AI cross-verifies with stored performance data before generating the report.
Temperature: Low, to ensure factual and consistent reporting.
RAG: Pulls from EduSavvy’s internal quiz database and cross-checks with KICD grading rubrics.
Negative Prompting: Do not use vague praise such as “your child is brilliant.”
