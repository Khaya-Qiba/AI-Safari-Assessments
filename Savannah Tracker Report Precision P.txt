Savannah Tracker Report: Precision Prompting Initiative
Introduction
In rural Kenya and Uganda, unclear health guidance can block access to care. Mothers living far from clinics need advice that matches their daily realities. Precision in prompting ensures that AI responses are practical, culturally suitable, and sensitive to local conditions. This approach builds trust and strengthens maternal health support.
Nutrition Advice (Prompt A)
Original Prompt: “Give nutrition tips for pregnant women.”
Rewritten Prompt:
"Act as a rural health nutrition expert. Provide three actionable, low-cost dietary adjustments for a pregnant woman in rural Kenya or Uganda. Focus on incorporating local staples like ugali or matooke with protein-rich additions (e.g., omena or legumes). Ensure the advice aligns with community food availability and budget constraints."
AIM Framework
Audience: Pregnant women in rural Kenya/Uganda
Instruction: Provide three low-cost, local-staple-based dietary adjustments
Method: Expert-level, locally sourced nutrition guidelines
MAP Framework
Model: Act as a rural health nutrition expert
Action: Suggest protein-rich additions to ugali/matooke
Parameters: Emphasize accessibility, affordability, and local availability
Key Improvement: Cultural alignment ensures that nutrition advice is practical and grounded in local diets.
Appointment Reminders (Prompt B)
Original Prompt: “Remind users about doctor visits.”
Rewritten Prompt:
"As a Community Health Assistant, generate a reminder for a prenatal check-up. Include a prompt for the user to account for local travel time (e.g., walking distance to the nearest clinic) and verify if the local Community Health Worker has indicated clinic availability this week. Remind the user to ensure M-Pesa funds are ready for transport costs."
AIM Framework
Audience: Expectant mothers in rural areas
Instruction: Generate a logistical reminder for prenatal visits
Method: Consideration of transit, budget, and local personnel
MAP Framework
Model: Community Health Assistant
Action: Request travel/budget planning and clinic verification
Parameters: Incorporate M-Pesa transport needs and local transit limitations
Key Improvement: Logistical context reduces missed appointments caused by travel or financial barriers.
Emergency Triage (Prompt C)
Original Prompt: “Tell me what to do if I feel unwell during pregnancy.”
Rewritten Prompt:
"Think step-by-step: 1. Ask the user to describe symptoms. 2. Compare symptoms against a provided list of 'Red Flag' conditions (e.g., severe bleeding, high fever, extreme swelling). 3. If a 'Red Flag' is present, advise immediate action: contact the nearest Community Health Worker or proceed to the clinic. 4. If symptoms are mild, suggest supportive care. Verifier Pattern: If you suggest the user go to the clinic, include a disclaimer that this is a screening tool, not a clinical diagnosis."
AIM Framework
Audience: Pregnant users needing immediate guidance
Instruction: Systematic triage of symptoms using a safety-first logic
Method: Step-by-step Chain-of-Thought with a mandatory disclaimer
MAP Framework
Model: Clinical triage assistant
Action: Validate symptoms against specific danger signs
Parameters: Prioritize immediate escalation for emergencies; provide a formal disclaimer
Key Improvement: Structured triage reduces the risk of unsafe or vague advice.
Reflection
Precision prompting shifts AI from a general information source to a practical partner in rural maternal care. Each rewritten prompt embeds cultural, financial, and logistical realities into the guidance. This approach ensures that mothers receive advice that is safe, affordable, and context-specific. The Savannah Tracker Initiative demonstrates how structured prompts can transform AI into a reliable tool for maternal survival in regions where distance and cost often block access to care.
